# Image Style Guide for Darsh Collections

This document defines the strict, consistent visual language required for all AI-generated images across the Darsh Collections digital catalogue. To maintain the illusion of a single, high-end cohesive photoshoot, all image generation prompts MUST incorporate the elements defined below.

## 1. Core Visual Directives

### Lighting
- **Soft Natural Window Light**: All lighting should emulate soft, diffused, natural daylight coming from large windows. 
- **Directional yet Soft**: Avoid harsh, direct flash or flat studio strobes. Shadows should be soft, wrapped, and elegant, revealing the folds and texture of the fabric without creating stark contrast lines.

### Backdrop & Environment
- **Palette Compliance**: Backgrounds must be drawn strictly from the brand palette: Ivory/Cream (`#FAF9F6`), Deep Maroon (`#4A1A23`), or Antique Gold (`#C5A059`).
- **Texture**: Environments should feature muted, textured walls (e.g., lime wash, soft plaster, traditional sandstone) or out-of-focus heritage architectural elements (e.g., a faint archway or carved wooden pillar). 
- **No Clutter**: The background must never compete with the saree.

### Photographic Style & Camera Settings
- **Editorial Fashion Catalogue**: Images must look like they belong in a premium fashion magazine or a high-end designer's lookbook.
- **Depth of Field**: Shallow depth of field (e.g., f/1.8 to f/2.8 aesthetic) to isolate the subject or the specific fabric detail, allowing the background to fall into a creamy bokeh.
- **Detail & Sharpness**: Extremely high detail on the fabric itself. Every thread, zari weave, and motif must be crisp and clearly defined.

### Color Grading
- **Warm, Rich, & Film-like**: A subtle cinematic color grade. Colors should be rich and saturated but maintain a sophisticated, slightly muted elegance. Avoid hyper-vibrant, digital, or HDR looks. Gold tones should look authentic and metallic, not neon yellow.

### Model Direction (When Applicable)
- **Aesthetic**: Elegant, modern-traditional Indian women.
- **Posing**: Natural, poised, and candid-style poses. Avoid stiff, overly posed, or artificial expressions. The focus should be on the drape and flow of the garment.
- **Anatomy**: Strict adherence to anatomical correctness. No distorted faces, eyes, or hands. Hands should be relaxed and naturally positioned.

## 2. Universal Prompt Prefix/Suffix
When generating images, use this base prompt modifier to ensure consistency:

> **Base Style Modifier**: "Editorial fashion photography, premium Indian saree boutique, soft natural diffused window light, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, rich warm cinematic color grade. High-end textile detail, crisp focus on fabric. Subtle backdrop in ivory, deep maroon, or antique gold. No text, no logos, no watermarks, anatomically correct, photorealistic masterpiece."

---

## 3. Image Categories & Specific Requirements

### Hero Images (16:9, Landscape)
- **Subject**: Full-bleed cinematic shots. The model should be positioned to one side (left or right) to allow negative space (in ivory or dark maroon) for headline text on the opposite side.
- **Vibe**: Sweeping, dramatic, and inviting.

### Collection Covers (4:5, Portrait)
- **Subject**: Artistic, atmospheric shots representing the *essence* of the collection (e.g., a bride for Bridal, a temple setting for Kanjivaram). 
- **Vibe**: Evocative and thematic.

### Saree Products (4:5 or 3:4, Portrait)
For each of the 24 sarees, we require three specific angles:
1. **The Full Drape**: Full body or 3/4 length shot showing how the saree falls.
2. **The Pallu / Border Close-up**: Tight shot on the shoulder or waist, highlighting the intricate zari work and border design.
3. **The Fabric Texture**: Macro shot of the weave, filling the frame entirely to show the silk/cotton grain and motif details.

### Lookbook / Occasions
- **Subject**: Styled shots showing the saree in context (e.g., a woman adjusting her jewelry, sitting elegantly on a traditional chair, or walking).
- **Vibe**: Lifestyle, aspirational, candid elegance.

### Know Your Weaves / Heritage (Editorial)
- **Subject**: Artisans at work, wooden handlooms, spools of silk thread, or extreme close-ups of the weaving process.
- **Vibe**: Authentic, documentary-style yet highly polished and editorial.

### Textures (Tileable / Backgrounds)
- **Subject**: Flat, edge-to-edge shots of raw silk, zari threads, or subtle woven patterns.
- **Vibe**: Minimalist, abstract, useful for UI overlays.

---

## 4. Strict Quality Control Rules
- **No Text**: The AI must not generate any text, brand names, or watermarks in the images.
- **No Artifacts**: Reject images with melted jewelry, distorted fabric patterns, or anatomically incorrect hands/faces.
- **Consistency**: If an image feels too bright, too modern/pop, or clashes with the Quiet Luxury aesthetic, it must be regenerated.
